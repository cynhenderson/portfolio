CDMX Trip

October 13 – 20, 2026

Tuesday, Oct 13: Arrival & Settling In

Afternoon — Check-In

Roma-Condesa Laidback Base

Arrive in the city and drop bags at ULIV Pulse Roma-Condesa. This area is tree-lined, very tranquil, close to the metro, and low on crime—perfect for putting Mom at ease as you settle in.

📍 ULIV Pulse on Maps

Late Lunch / Dinner

Low-Stress Tacos

Keep it incredibly easy for the first night. Walk to either Tacos Hola El Güero (famous for their guisados in clay pots) or Taquería El Jarocho. Both are casual, legendary, and right in the neighborhood.

📍 Tacos Hola El Güero

Evening Stroll

Parque México

Take a gentle, easy walk through Parque México or Parque España to help Mom unwind and get a feel for the neighborhood's beautiful art deco architecture.

Wednesday, Oct 14: Coyoacán Stroll

Morning — Plazas & Coffee

South CDMX Cobblestones

Head south to Coyoacán. Take a slow walk through the beautiful, bohemian plazas: Jardín Hidalgo and Jardín Centenario. Grab a traditional coffee at Café El Jarocho to start the day.

Afternoon — Museums

Frida Kahlo Trotsky

Visit the Frida Kahlo Museum (Casa Azul) or the Leon Trotsky Museum.

⚠️ Make sure to book tickets well in advance for Casa Azul!

Late Afternoon — Churros

Sweet Treat

Wander the cobblestone streets and pick up some fresh churros before heading back to Condesa for the evening.

Thursday, Oct 15: Centro Histórico & Artesanías

Morning — Mercado de Artesanías La Ciudadela

Folk Art Souvenirs

Head toward the historic center early. La Ciudadela is the premier spot for authentic, high-quality folk art from all over Mexico. Intricate textiles, Talavera ceramics, and vibrant alebrijes. Take your time browsing!

📍 La Ciudadela on Maps

Lunch — Thomson Reuters

Colleagues

Meet up with your colleagues for lunch at or around the Thomson Reuters office.

Afternoon — MUNAL

Classic Mexican Art Architecture

Walk to MUNAL (Museo Nacional de Arte). The building itself is one of the most stunning in the city, housing an incredible collection of Mexican art.

📍 MUNAL on Maps

Friday, Oct 16: Chapultepec & Condesa

Morning — Castillo de Chapultepec

Arrive Early Hilltop Views

Head into Bosque de Chapultepec right when it opens to beat the heat and the crowds. The walk up to the castle is paved but on an incline. The views of Reforma from the top are unmatched.

📍 Castillo on Maps

Afternoon — Museo de Arte Moderno (MAM)

Modern Art

After a casual lunch in or near the park, walk over to MAM. The architecture of the building and the sculpture garden are just as impressive as the exhibits inside.

📍 MAM on Maps

Late Afternoon — Condesa Shopping

Boutiques

Walk back into Condesa and spend the afternoon winding down by popping into local boutiques around Amsterdam Avenue.

Dinner — Jowong

Korean Fusion Banchan

Head to Calle Pachuca for dinner at Jowong. Elevated Korean-Mexican fusion with an incredible array of banchan. A great change of pace for the palate!

📍 Jowong on Maps

Saturday, Oct 17: Family Day

All Day

Family

Reserved entirely for spending time with family.

Sunday, Oct 18: Family Day

All Day

Family

Reserved entirely for spending time with family.

Monday, Oct 19: Roma Norte & La Laguna

Morning — Roma Norte Shopping

Pingüino Sara Casa

Since museums are closed on Mondays, today is all about the neighborhoods. Stroll through Roma Norte and make sure to visit Pingüino and Sara Casa for unique clothing and home pieces.

Lunch — Merendero Biarritz

Retro Diner

Grab lunch at Merendero Biarritz. Great food in a fun, classic retro atmosphere.

📍 Merendero Biarritz

Afternoon — La Laguna

Doctores Design Studios

Head over to the edge of Roma into the Doctores neighborhood to visit La Laguna. This beautifully restored 1920s textile factory is packed with independent design studios, ceramic workshops, and coffee roasters.

📍 La Laguna on Maps

Evening — Farewell Dinner

Last Night

Enjoy your last evening with a lovely dinner somewhere in the Roma or Condesa neighborhood before heading home tomorrow.

Tuesday, Oct 20: Departure

Morning — Coffee & Pack

Pastries

Grab a final coffee and some pan dulce in the neighborhood, pack up, and check out of ULIV Pulse.

Head to the Airport

Safe Travels

Order a rideshare to the airport. Make sure to account for CDMX traffic!
